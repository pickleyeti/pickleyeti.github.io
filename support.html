Support

Questions, bug reports, or privacy requests: email support@pickleyeti.com. Please say which game, device and OS version you are using. I reply within a few days.

Barstool Brawl

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad consent?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the game (shown in regions where it is required). On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

An ad didn't give me my reward.

Rewards are only granted when the ad network confirms you watched the ad to the end. If that keeps failing, email me with the time it happened.

The screen wobbles too much.

Turn off Settings → Drunk vision.
