Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026. Applies to every game published by Pickle Yeti, the studio name of independent developer Ildyakov ("I", "me") on the App Store and Google Play, currently: Barstool Brawl.

Summary

  • The games have no accounts and don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Crash reports and anonymous play statistics help fix bugs (Unity Gaming Services).
  • Game progress and settings stay on your device. Progress can also sync between your own devices through your iCloud or Google Play Games account.
  • The games are free and show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. An optional one-time purchase removes the ads shown between rounds.
  • You can refuse personalised ads and tracking. The games work the same either way.

Data stored on your device

Progress, unlocked items, scores and settings (sound, visual effects) are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to me. Deleting the game deletes the local copy.

Progress sync

To keep your progress when you change or reinstall devices, the games keep a small copy of it (levels reached, best scores, unlocked items; no personal information) in your own iCloud account on iOS, or in Google Play Games saved games on Android. Apple or Google stores it under their terms; I can't read it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for the game in iOS Settings, or don't sign in to Play Games.

Crash reports and game statistics

The games use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to send crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the error itself) and, outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, anonymous play statistics (sessions, levels reached, app version) so I can fix bugs and see which parts are played. They are not linked to your name or used for advertising. See Unity's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases (such as "No Ads") are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. I never see your payment details; the store only tells the game what you own, so it can be restored on a new device with "Restore purchases".

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games use the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers: advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), app-set IDAdvertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
IP address and the approximate location derived from itShowing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (impressions, taps), app launchesMeasurement, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (crash logs, performance data)Keeping the SDK working

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. I don't receive data that identifies you personally. AdMob only gives me aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information through the same Privacy options screen when it is shown.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Leaderboards

If a game offers an online leaderboard, it uses Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android). Your scores are then shown under your Game Center or Play Games nickname, under Apple's or Google's terms. I don't collect free-text names.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and Barstool Brawl is rated 18+ / Mature. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used a game and want data removed, contact me.

Data retention and your rights

I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete or export. To exercise your rights over data processed by Google (access, deletion, objection), use the links above or Google's My Ad Center. For any question, email support@pickleyeti.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a game adds a new service, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
