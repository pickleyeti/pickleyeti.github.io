Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026. Applies to every game published by Pickle Yeti, the studio name of independent developer Ildyakov ("I", "me") on the App Store and Google Play, including Barstool Brawl and Grubble: Endless Mine. Features and platforms vary by game.

Summary

  • The games have no accounts and don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Crash reports and play statistics associated with a generated identifier help fix bugs (Unity Gaming Services).
  • Game progress and settings stay on your device. Progress can also sync between your own devices through your iCloud or Google Play Games account.
  • The games are free and show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. An optional one-time No Ads purchase removes automatic ads between rounds or runs. Optional rewarded ads remain available.
  • You can refuse personalised ads and tracking. The games work the same either way.

Data stored on your device

Progress, unlocked items, scores and settings (sound, visual effects) are saved locally on your device. Purchase transaction identifiers are also saved to prevent duplicate delivery of currency packs. Deleting the game deletes the local copy; platform cloud saves and purchase records may remain.

Progress sync

To keep your progress when you change or reinstall devices, the games keep a small copy of it (levels reached, best scores, unlocked items and, where offered, gold and diamond balances and purchase transaction identifiers) in your own iCloud account on iOS, or in Google Play Games saved games on Android. Apple or Google stores it under their terms. This is not a developer-hosted player account. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for the game in iOS Settings, or don't sign in to Play Games.

Crash reports and game statistics

The games use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to send crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the error itself) and, outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, play statistics (sessions, levels reached, app version and a generated analytics identifier) so I can fix bugs and see which parts are played. The games do not supply your name to Unity or use these statistics for advertising. Generated identifiers can distinguish installations without requiring your name. See Unity's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. I do not receive your card or bank details. The store provides product, purchase-status and transaction information so the game can deliver purchases and avoid granting the same transaction twice.

Grubble offers permanent miner unlocks and No Ads, plus consumable gold and diamond packs. Restore Purchases restores eligible permanent purchases using the same store account. Consumable packs are not restored through that button; any remaining currency is part of the saved game progress and may sync through platform cloud saves when available. No Ads removes automatic interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games use the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers: advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), app-set IDAdvertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
IP address and the approximate location derived from itShowing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (impressions, taps), app launchesMeasurement, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (crash logs, performance data)Keeping the SDK working

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. I don't receive data that identifies you personally. AdMob only gives me aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information through the same Privacy options screen when it is shown.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Leaderboards

If a game offers an online leaderboard, it uses Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android). Your scores are then shown under your Game Center or Play Games nickname, under Apple's or Google's terms. I don't collect free-text names.

Children

The games are not designed specifically for children. Each game’s store listing provides its own age rating; Barstool Brawl’s adult themes do not apply to Grubble. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used a game and want data removed, contact me.

Data retention and your rights

Local progress remains until you delete it or uninstall the game. Cloud saves and store purchase records are retained under Apple’s or Google’s settings and policies. Advertising, diagnostics and analytics data are retained under the relevant provider’s policies. If you email support, I receive your email address, message and any details you choose to send, and use them to handle your request. You may contact me to request access to or deletion of information I hold, subject to applicable requirements. For data processed by Apple, Google or Unity, use their privacy controls and policies linked on this page, including Google's My Ad Center. For any question, email support@pickleyeti.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a game adds a new service, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
